Subscribe to Calendar Updates

Get SNAP events in your own Google Calendar. Check the categories you want, then subscribe. Each category opens a Google Calendar page where you confirm with one click. Google adds one calendar per click, so subscribing to several categories opens one tab per category (your browser may ask you to allow pop-ups for this site).
